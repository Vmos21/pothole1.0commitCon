The Severity-Only "Crisscross Trap"

Greedy algorithms fix the worst individual potholes first, but cause work crews to drive back-and-forth across the district. A crew burns valuable operating hours and diesel fuel crisscrossing wards, sealing fewer total patches per workday.

hourglass_disabled
The First-Come-First-Served Fallacy

Queue-based scheduling treats hairline cracks on quiet cul-de-sacs with the same dispatch priority as wheel-shattering craters on high-volume bus routes simply because someone called 311 five minutes earlier. This inflates civic liability.

auto_graph
The RoadWise Pareto Frontier

By clustering localized clusters along arterial segments, RoadWise achieves 95.5% high-severity resolution while simultaneously reducing vehicle travel distance by 56% compared to traditional queue models.
