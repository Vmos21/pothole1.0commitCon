Civic Explainability

Why Municipalities Don't Fix "First-Come, First-Served"

Pothole repairs require heavy machinery, asphalt distribution logistics, and traffic control permits. To prevent life-threatening accidents and maximize public tax dollars, the RoadWise AI Engine dynamically weights four critical criteria for every submitted ticket.

balance

Equitable Governance: Algorithmic triage removes political bias, routing municipal resources directly where safety threats and transit congestion are most urgent.

straighten

Pothole Severity & Depth

AI calculates crater diameter and millimeter depth to identify immediate hazards that cause tire punctures, wheel rim shears, or loss of two-wheeler vehicle control.

directions_bus

Commuter & Transit Density

Cross-referenced with live municipal telemetry. Dedicated bus corridors, hospital access lanes, and school crossing zones take top triage priority.

alt_route

Road Classification Index

Major arterials and expressways carrying high-speed vehicles are addressed ahead of quiet cul-de-sacs to eliminate chain-reaction freeway accidents.

layers

Subsurface Recurrence

Recurring craters highlight underlying stormwater pipe leaks or foundation saturation, signaling engineering crews to perform complete sub-base overhauls.
